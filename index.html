SOP-MHG-T1

Tier 1: Basic Site Protocol & Logistics

1. Clinical Workflow Steps

  • Perform standard venipuncture following GLAD sample kit configurations.
  • Centrifugation Requirement: Spin blood collection tubes immediately post-draw using standard local laboratory parameters to separate target fluid pathways.
  • Do not leave raw samples ambient or unspun on lab racks.

2. Logistics & Daily Shipping Pathway

Because Tier 1 sites do not have access to ultra-low freezer storage setups, processed tubes cannot be batched locally on site.

📦

Royal Mail Daily Post Pipeline:

Pack freshly centrifuged specimens directly into the pre-paid, clinical-grade Royal Mail SafeBox container supplied in your kit. Ship to King's College London (KCL) central lab intake on the exact same afternoon.

SOP-MHG-T2

Tier 2: Intermediate Site Protocol & Logistics

1. Clinical Laboratory Processing

  • Execute target venipuncture collections as instructed in study pack materials.
  • Centrifuge raw tubes immediately to separate plasma/serum components.
  • Processing/Storage Requirement: Aliquot fluids into matrix storage tubes, label via standard barcodes, and immediately transfer into site freezers at -20°C or -80°C.

2. Cold-Chain Logistics & Batch Rules

Samples must be preserved in a continuous frozen matrix locally. Do not post frozen derivatives via standard mail channels.

❄️

Dry Ice Courier Shipping Rule:

Accumulate aliquots continuously within your local repository. Once your specific repository milestone achieves its designated minimum of 10 samples, notify coordinate support to dispatch a specialized medical cold-chain courier to move materials to KCL on dry ice.

SOP-MHG-T3

Tier 3: Advanced Site Protocol & Logistics

1. Cellular Isolation Requirements (PBMC)

  • Perform phlebotomy and spin down matrices using specified parameters.
  • Advanced PBMC Requirement: Immediately transfer to sterile laminar flow isolation hoods. Process and isolate live Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells (PBMCs) following exact study density gradient separation checklists.
  • Cryopreserve fractions step-down style and anchor securely into designated -80°C biorepository slots.

2. Advanced Cold-Chain Batch Shipping

🧊

Dry Ice Courier Logistics Workflow:

To preserve cellular viability across freezing cycles, all samples must remain at critical sub-zero thresholds. Batch samples locally on site and trigger specialized cold-chain courier transfer over to KCL on deep dry ice matrices upon achieving the 10-sample collection threshold.
