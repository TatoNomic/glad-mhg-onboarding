Lymphocyte Fraction Layer

PBMC Core

Execute specialized density gradient centrifuge profiles (Ficoll matrices) inside laminar hoods to isolate and capture the live mononuclear layer.

Processing Run-Sheet Checklist

  • Execute full 5-tube fractionation profile exactly as outlined in Tier 2 lanes.
  • Run Ficoll-gradient separation cycles within specialized biological safety cabinets.
  • Carefully pipette off the white mononuclear cell layer interface (Buffy coat).
  • Cryopreserve cellular fractions. Retain securely inside liquid nitrogen vapor cells.
⚠️

Critical Quality Safeguard Rule

Tier 1 settings lack sub-zero repository equipment. Samples must be centrifuged to stabilize the matrix components, then dispatched immediately. Holding aliquoted tubes at ambient temperatures on your open lab benches will trigger irreversible degradation of data-omics targets.

❄️

Dry Ice Batch Shipping Management

For Tier 2 and Tier 3 hubs: Store local collections systematically. Once your tracking registry logs a **minimum accumulation threshold of 10 samples**, schedule central medical transit on deep dry ice to clear your inventory footprint back to KCL.
