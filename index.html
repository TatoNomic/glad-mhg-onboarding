3 Logistics Pathways & Pipeline Management

Review logistics pipelines based on site setup rules.

Postal Pathway (Tier 1)
Frequency: Immediate Daily

Royal Mail Post-Centrifugation Return

Tier 1 sites possess centrifugation capabilities to spin down blood collections immediately post-draw. However, because long-term freezer storage is unavailable, spun samples must be packaged into pre-paid Royal Mail SafeBox containers and posted back to KCL on the same day.

Required: Local Centrifuge • Royal Mail SafeBox Packing Materials
Cold Chain Pathway (Tiers 2 & 3)
Frequency: Threshold Batched

Dry Ice Batch Shipping Pipeline

Tier 2 and Tier 3 facilities centrifuge, process, and completely freeze derivatives locally. Samples accumulate inside designated site freezers. Once your specific repository reaches its designated accumulation target, a specialized medical cold-chain courier is dispatched to move items on dry ice.
